# Project Guidelines & Conventions

## Branding and Naming Conventions
- **"ebm-papst" Casing Rule**: The company and brand name must **always** be spelled with the exact casing **"ebm-papst"** (all lowercase with hyphen).
  - Always use: `ebm-papst`
  - Never use: `EBM-Papst`, `Ebm-papst`, `EBM-PAPST`, or `ebmpapst`
  - This applies to user-facing copy, generated AI answers, system prompts, greetings, report titles, UI templates, and documentation.
